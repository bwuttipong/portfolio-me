# bar.md — Teardown of linear.app (the bar)

Five to seven mechanisms, each checkable by looking. Judges must verify these against rendered output, not code.

1. **Three type sizes per screen.** Hero headline is ~5x body size; the rest of the page lives in two sizes: body copy and micro-labels. Any fourth competing size breaks the bar.
2. **Monochrome base, one accent, ≤2 accent uses per screen.** Near-everything is one neutral hue; the accent appears at most twice per viewport, always on something interactive or a single emphasis.
3. **Product evidence as the only visuals.** Every visual is a real or faithful artifact — UI dioramas, issue rows, code diffs, figure labels ("Fig 0.1", "ENG-2076"). No decorative stock, no floating icons, no abstract blobs.
4. **Micro metadata texture.** Compact monospace-style labels (IDs, figure numbers, dates) sit beside content, giving a "real system" texture without adding type sizes.
5. **Structure by whitespace and thin rules, not boxes.** Sections separate with generous space and 1px lines; no card shadows, no nested boxes, flat surfaces throughout.
6. **Motion settles, never performs.** Content appears in place — no scroll-jacking, no bounce; if a transition exists it resolves in one direction and under 400ms. Nothing animates for decoration.
7. **Rhythm: one open hero, dense body.** Above the fold is at least 40% open space; below it, information is dense — compact rows, tight leading, lists that read like a working tool, not marketing blurbs.

## Copy bar (from SlopMonster)

8. **Visible copy scores 5/5 on deslop.py.** No AI vocabulary, no rule-of-three cadence, no em-dash pile-ups. Meaning intact; no invented facts.
